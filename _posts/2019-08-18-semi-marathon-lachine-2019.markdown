# First race alone in Quebec

At this time, because I'm writting this post in 2024, I'm still running alone most of the time. I do have friends who run regularly but none in Montreal. I register to this semi-marathon as a training race before the marathon of Quebec city for the next autumn.

I also remember that wasn't exploring much this part of Montreal island by bike and therefore decided to go to the start of thre race by bus. This was a fun ride as the bus was full of people like me, dressed to suffer wih their bib on their chest.

## During the race

Nice weather, I started fast but the wasn't holding the speed very long... Puting the energy into finishing the race mainly.

## After the race

An ok performance of 2:06:58, nothing exceptional really, but I do remember to have enjoy the moment.

I surely did not put all my legs into that run, because later I went on the other side of the city by bike to join friends for a bbq. Beers and sausages, that's the motivation.
